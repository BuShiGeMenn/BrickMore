\section{Conclusion}
BrickForma turns LLM-authored parametric programs into checked voxel-to-brick targets and uses them to fine-tune Qwen3-1.7B for \texttt{Text-to-Brick} assembly. On 525 matched prompts spanning five description levels, its raw outputs lead on all three text--image alignment metrics while keeping the mean invalid-placement ratio at 0.79\%. Combined with exact-cover MILP refinement, which enforces stud connectivity while preserving the voxel union, this division of labor offers a practical path toward constructive 3D spatial reasoning in compact models.
