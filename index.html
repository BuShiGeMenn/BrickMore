Abstract

Generating physically buildable toy brick structures from text prompts requires both high spatial resolution and global structural connectivity. Prior autoregressive methods such as BrickGPT operate on 20×20×20 grids with 8 cuboid brick types and rely on iterative physics-simulator rollbacks during inference. We introduce BrickMore, a local small-language-model framework that scales text-to-brick generation to 48×48×48 resolution (13.8× larger volume) across 20 standard LEGO® brick types. By pairing single-pass local autoregressive decoding (BrickGPT-Qwen3-1.7B, requiring only 3.56 GiB VRAM) with a deterministic conformal bridge retiling algorithm, BrickMore eliminates inference-time rollbacks and unifies multi-leg and cantilevered structures into a single grounded stud-connected component, achieving a 99.12% pass rate across 80,000 models with zero brick collisions.

Method Overview

Instead of expensive token-level physics rollbacks during LLM decoding, BrickMore decouples high-resolution volumetric generation from deterministic structural interlocking. Click any stage below to inspect its role in the 3D viewer.

Stage 2A (Conformal Bridge Retiling): Identifies disconnected stud components (shown in red in Raw Draft mode) and re-tiles local brick seams across adjacent layers to lock multi-leg tables, chairs, and grand pianos into 1 grounded component in 0.13s–1.42s.
