\begin{abstract}
Geometric spatial reasoning lets designers infer dimensions and part layouts from sparse descriptions. Text-to-brick generation challenges language models to make those inferences and express them as discrete placements that interlock. BrickForma transfers geometric knowledge from large language models into Qwen3-1.7B through executable training-data generators. LLM-assisted parametric programs synthesize 80,000 voxel shapes across 21 categories, each with five description levels. Voxel-to-brick tiling, local repair, and geometric checks convert these shapes into training targets. We fine-tune on caption--placement pairs and use mixed-integer programming to reconstruct raw predictions as single stud-connected exact covers. On 265 matched prompts, raw BrickForma outputs achieve the highest mean VQAScore (0.1079), PE (0.1572), and SigLIP2 (0.0565), with a mean invalid-placement ratio of 0.81\%. In a separate 110-pair audit, the number of single-component assemblies increases from 18 to 110 after reconstruction, with equal occupied voxel unions after translation normalization.
\end{abstract}
