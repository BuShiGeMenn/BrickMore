Contact-based gravity screening.We model an 8mm
stud spacing, a 9.6mm brick height, and a mass of 1g per
occupied stud-brick cell. Each overlapping stud contributes
four vertical contact points at the corners of a square with a
half-widthof0.25stud.Thehorizontalgroundprovidesonly
compression, at the corners of ground-level brick footprints
insetby0.05stud.Alinearprogram(LP)seeksverticalforce
balance and balance of the two gravity-induced moments
for every brick, and we verify the resulting contact forces
against these equations. Once the final assembly passes, the
same check is applied, from bottom to top, to the bricks up
to each completed layer. Each stud-connected component of
such a prefix is checked independently on the unanchored
ground.
Tippingmarginandclutchdemand.Thetippingmargin
is the minimum signed inward distance from the horizontal
projection of the center of mass to the edges of the convex
hull of the ground contacts. We convert the margin from
studs to meters and reject zero or negative margins within
numericaltolerance.Ifthecompression-onlyLPisinfeasible
afterthischeck,asecondLPpermitstensionatinternalstud
contacts and minimizes the largest total tensile force over
the four contact points of any single stud. We report this
minimum required clutch force in newtons per stud; actual
clutchcapacityisnotcalibrated.Averifiedcompression-only
solution counts as a gravity-screening pass, while solutions
that require tensile support are recorded separately.
