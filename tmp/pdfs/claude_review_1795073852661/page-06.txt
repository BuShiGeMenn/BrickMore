5.6 Evaluation Boundaries
Our benchmark compares delivered exports from systems
that differ in inference settings and part libraries. Descrip-
tion levels differ in both semantic detail and token length,
so PE truncates 121 prompts and SigLIP2 truncates 209
(Appendix C). Uniform yellow rendering isolates geometric
form and layout but does not assess color fidelity. Finally,
the paired refinement audit covers only delivered pairs, so
unsuccessful solver attempts are excluded from its denomi-
nator.
6 Limitations and Discussion
Our experiments evaluate the data-construction and fine-
tuning pipeline as a whole. Isolating the contribution of
LLM-authored generators relative to hand-crafted procedu-
ralbaselinesunderamatchedtrainingbudgetremainsfuture
work.Moreover,datasetreleasechecksverifygroundingand
coverage, and inference-time refinement enforces a single
stud-connected component, but physical stability and feasi-
ble assembly ordering require dedicated structural measure-
ments. Finally, the integrated dataset release and the fine-
tuning run record their sample counts separately (Table 1).
7 Conclusion
BrickForma turns LLM-authored parametric programs into
checked voxel-to-brick targets and uses them to fine-tune
Qwen3-1.7B forText-to-Brickassembly. On 525
matched prompts spanning five description levels, its raw
outputsleadonallthreetext–imagealignmentmetricswhile
keeping the mean invalid-placement ratio at 0.79%. Com-
bined with exact-cover MILP refinement, which enforces
stud connectivity while preserving the voxel union, this di-
visionoflaboroffersapracticalpathtowardconstructive3D
spatial reasoning in compact models.
