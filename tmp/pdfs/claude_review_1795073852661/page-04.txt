Forallotherdrafts,weisolatethesemanticshapeproposal
by forming the in-bounds voxel unionCof all placements
withasupportedfootprint(countingoverlapsonce)andhand

exact cover ofCthat forms a single stud-connected compo-
nent, under time limits of 12, 60, and 180 seconds, and an
independent audit then checks placement validity, coverage,
and connectivity. Because the cover is exact, a refined lay-
out occupies exactly the voxel setCderived from its draft.
If all time limits are exhausted, the pipeline keeps the raw
