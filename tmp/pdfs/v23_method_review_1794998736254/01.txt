Abstract
Geometric spatial reasoning underlies applications from
roboticstoautomateddesign,yettheabilityoflargelanguage
models (LLMs) to turn a verbal description into a physically
connectedthree-dimensionalassemblyremainslargelyunder-
explored. Brick assembly is a demanding constructive probe
of this ability: a short, underspecified prompt leaves dimen-
sions,proportions,andpartlayoutimplicit,andavalidanswer
must match the semantic intent of the prompt while complet-
ing this geometry with discrete pieces that interlock through
studs.WestudythissettingastheText-to-Bricktaskand
presentBrickForma,aframeworkthatbuildstrainingdatafor
a compact model using LLM-assisted parametric programs.
Parametric programs written with large language model as-
sistance generate 80,000 source voxel models across 21 cat-
egories on a483 grid, each described at five levels of detail,
