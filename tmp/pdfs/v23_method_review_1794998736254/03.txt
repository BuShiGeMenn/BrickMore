3 Dataset Construction
3.1 Parametric Voxel Generation
To crystallize broad geometric priors into explicit supervi-
sion, GPT-6.1 Sol assists in authoring analytic parametric
generator programs across 21 object categories, each par-
titioned into 40 structural families (47–48 instances per
family in each 40,000-model shard). For every instance, a
planning module samples independent design parameters—
governingcategory-specificcomponents,proportions,open-
ings, and support structures—and deterministically raster-
izes the geometry onto a483 grid (Y=0ground contact)
while deriving L1–L5 captions from the same underlying
plan. Rather than applying random cell deletion, cavity fill-
ing, or post-hoc bridges, density adjustments regenerate the
entire shape from its continuous parameters until automated
checks confirm 750–1,250 occupied voxels and a single six-
neighbor connected component. Exact and canonical oc-
cupancy hashes across 48 axis and sign transforms verify
zero cross-shard duplicates, with geometry plans and ver-
sion identifiers preserved alongside each record (Figure 2,
whereshape(params)denotes the analytic shape and
emit(x,y,z)records occupied voxel coordinates).
BrickForma-48 (archived underBrickMore-48in the
accompanying dataset and checkpoint releases) pairs each
shape with five bilingual descriptions. L1 names the object
and its primary parts, whereas L2–L5 progressively layer
structural, proportional, appearance, and viewpoint details
onto the exact same source geometry (see Appendix A for a
birdhouse example).
