Contact-based gravity screening.We model 8mm stud
spacing, 9.6mm brick height, and 1g per occupied stud-
brick cell. Each overlapping stud contributes four vertical
contactpointsatthecornersofasquarewithhalf-width0.25
stud. The horizontal ground can only provide compression
at the corners of ground-level brick footprints inset by 0.05
stud. A linear program (LP) seeks vertical force balance
and balance of the two gravity-induced moments for every
brick. We verify the resulting contact forces against these
equations.Afterthefinalassemblypasses,thesamecheckis
appliedtothebricksthrougheachcompletedlayer,bottomto
top. Each stud-connected component of a prefix is checked
independently on the unanchored ground.
Tippingmarginandclutchdemand.Thetippingmargin
is the minimum signed inward distance from the horizontal
center-of-mass projection to the edges of the convex hull of
the ground contacts. We convert the margin from studs to
meters and reject zero or negative margins within numerical
tolerance. If the compression-only LP is infeasible after this
check,asecondLPpermitstensionatinternalstudcontacts.
It minimizes the largest total tensile force across the four
contact points of any single stud. We report this minimum
required clutch force in newtons per stud. Actual clutch ca-
pacity is uncalibrated. A verified compression-only solution
qualifiesforagravity-screeningpass,whilesolutionsrequir-
ing tensile support are recorded separately.
