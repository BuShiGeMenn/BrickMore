5.2 Main Results
Across all 525 prompts, BrickForma’s raw outputs achieve
the highest mean VQAScore (0.0866), PE (0.1568), and
SigLIP2 (0.0448) (Table 3), outperforming BrickGPT on
384, 337, and 327 prompts, respectively. All six paired
alignment comparisons remain statistically significant un-
der Holm correction (Appendix C). On placement valid-
ity, BrickGPT achieves the lowest meanPinv (0.00%) via
physics-aware rollback, followed by BrickForma (0.79%)
and BrickNet (18.42%), with zero-invalid-placement counts
of 525, 331, and 139, respectively (Figure 15). Figures 16
and 17 visualize pooled means and score distributions.

5.4 Raw versus Refined Outputs
To quantify the impact of exact-cover reconstruction, we
examine 110 raw/refined pairs from an earlier 114-prompt
comparison sharing identical prompts and model identifiers
(Table 5). Refinement eliminates invalid placements (Pinv
drops from 0.1897% to 0.0000%) and increases mean part
count from 178.7 to 421.2. VQAScore and PE remain es-
sentially unchanged, whereas SigLIP2 shifts from 0.1379 to
0.1261.
