We present BrickForma, which uses executable programs
to generate training examples for a compact text-to-brick
model. With GPT-6.1 Sol assistance, we develop paramet-
ric programs that vary object proportions, part dimensions,
openings, and support arrangements to synthesize voxel
shapesinbatches.Eachprogramspecifiesthestructureofan
object family and exposes its geometric degrees of freedom
as parameters. The source collection contains 80,000 mod-
els across 21 categories on a483 grid. Each model is paired
with five levels of description that progressively introduce
structural,geometric,proportional,andappearanceinforma-
tion. The resulting approximately 400,000 caption records
link concise requests and detailed specifications to shared
geometry, providing supervision for prompts with different
amounts of geometric detail.
