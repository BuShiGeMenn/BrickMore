A complete steam locomotive: a long round boiler with rivet bands, a front smokebox door, a tall chimney with a cap, a steam dome and a whistle, an open cab with a roof and rear bunker plates, side rods over six wheels, a cowcatcher and buffers at the front, and a taillight at the rear.
一辆完整的蒸汽机车:长圆锅炉带铆钉箍带,前面烟箱门,高烟囱带帽,有汽包和汽笛,开式驾驶室带车顶、后部是煤仓板,侧杆横跨六轮,前面排障器与缓冲器,尾部还有尾灯。
