A full castle turret: a circular shaft two studs thick rising ten layers, an arched doorway at the base, three staggered arrow slits up the wall, a machicolation ring at the top, crenellated battlements, a conical roof with a finial stud, and a banner on one side.
一座完整的城堡塔楼:圆形塔身厚两粒、拔地十层,底部有拱形门洞,墙上三处错落的箭孔一路向上,顶部有坠垛口环,城齿雉堞,锥形塔顶带顶尖饰凸点,一侧还有一面旗帜。
